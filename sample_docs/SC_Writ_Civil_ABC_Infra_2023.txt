JUDGMENT
SANJIV KHANNA, J.

1. The present Writ Petition under Article 32 of the Constitution of India challenges the arbitrary cancellation of tender notice dated 14th January 2023 and the subsequent awarding of the EPC Contract to Respondent No. 3 on nomination basis.
2. FACTUAL MATRIX: The Petitioner emerged as the lowest bidder (L-1) quoting INR 842 Crores. The State cancelled the tender citing administrative exigency and awarded it to Respondent No. 3 at INR 895 Crores.
3. SUBMISSIONS: Petitioner contends violation of Article 14 and Article 19(1)(g). State claims emergency procurement under Karnataka Transparency in Public Procurements Act, 1999.
4. REASONING: Decision-making must be free from arbitrariness. No contemporaneous emergency records exist. Public exchequer cannot be compromised.
5. FINAL ORDER: In view of the above, the Writ Petition is ALLOWED. Impugned cancellation dated 10th May 2023 is quashed and set aside.
